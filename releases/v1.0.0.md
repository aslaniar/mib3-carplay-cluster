# v1.0.0 — Path B, field-verified

First public release. The phone's own H.264 reaches the instrument cluster: no decode, no
re-encode, no second generation of compression.

## Platform

- VW MIB3, LG, train `MOI3_EU_VW_R8830L_MU0883`, part `3G8919360AR`
- cluster 1280×480, fed by `multi-screen-server` over RTP/MPEG-TS
- aarch64, glibc ≤ 2.23, no CRT in the shipped image

Other trains are untested. Nothing here is persistent: `/tmp` and `/dev/shm` only, and a
reboot clears it.

## Requirements

Root on the unit. The shim is loaded into a live `carplay` process; this repository does not
include or describe any method of obtaining that privilege.

## Known limitation

The ECU's receiver is built for the car's own stream — about 10 fps at 190 kbps. The phone
sends roughly 2.5 Mbps, and that ratio is where the remaining light artifacts come from.
Attacking it by starving the receiver is not the answer: the periodic recovery-point pump
is load-bearing, and removing it produces "first frame only, then nothing" while every
counter still looks healthy.
